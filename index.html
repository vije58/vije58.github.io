About Me

Artist, Designer, Educator

My work explores displacement, memory, and belonging through painting, installation, interactive media, and digital design. Rooted in my experience and immigrant, I create spaces- both physical and digital- where vulnerability and resilience coexist. As an educator, I am committed to fostering inclusive, hands - on learning that connects art, technology, and human experience.
