Between Me and You

Mixed media installation

Between Me and You explores how closeness and patience allow us to reveal ourselves to one another. Three layers of painted fabric conceal a painting, lifting gradually as a viewer approaches and stays. Each layer reveals more of what lies beneath, echoing the slow unfolding of trust. The work invites viewers to consider how sustained presence creates space for someone to open up and share their memories, identity, and past.

Year
2025
Material
Mixed media, oil on canvas, fabric, wood dwell, sensor, Arduino board.
Dimension
Variable
